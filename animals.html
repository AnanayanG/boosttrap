Lion

Lions are strong, social big cats known for living in groups called prides. They are skilled hunters, with females doing most of the hunting while males protect the territory. Lions are often called the “king of the jungle,” even though they usually live in grasslands and savannas.

Tiger

Tigers are the largest species of big cats and are easily recognized by their orange fur with black stripes. They are solitary animals that prefer to hunt alone, using stealth and power to catch prey. Tigers are excellent swimmers and often cool off in rivers and lakes.

Penguin

Penguins are flightless birds adapted to life in cold regions, especially Antarctica. They use their flippers to swim quickly underwater and catch fish. Penguins live in large colonies and rely on teamwork to survive harsh climates, especially during breeding seasons.

Peacock

Peacocks are famous for their bright, colorful tail feathers, which males spread out in a fan shape to attract females. They belong to the pheasant family and are known for their loud calls and graceful movements. Their shimmering feathers create beautiful patterns when they display.

Parrot

Parrots are intelligent, brightly colored birds found mostly in tropical forests. They are known for their ability to mimic sounds and even human speech. Parrots have strong curved beaks for cracking nuts and seeds, and many species can live for several decades.
